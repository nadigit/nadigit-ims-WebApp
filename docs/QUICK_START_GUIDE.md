# Nadigit IMS - Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to Nadigit IMS!

This quick start guide will help you get up and running with the essential features of Nadigit IMS in just a few minutes.

---

## 🚀 First Steps

### 1. Logging In
- Navigate to the login page
- Enter your username and password
- Click **Login**

### 2. Understanding Your Dashboard
After logging in, you'll see the dashboard with:
- **Total Orders**: Number of orders in your system
- **Today's Revenue**: Money made today
- **Total Customers**: Number of registered customers
- **Total Products**: Products in your inventory

---

## 📦 Essential Inventory Tasks

### Adding Your First Product

1. Go to **Inventory** → **Products**
2. Click **New**
3. Fill in:
   - Product name
   - Category
   - Price
   - Stock quantity
   - Warehouse location
4. Click **Save**

**💡 Tip**: Use categories to organize products. Create categories first in **Inventory** → **Categories**.

### Managing Stock

- **View Stock**: Products table shows current quantities
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Products below threshold are highlighted in red
- **Update Stock**: Edit product to change stock levels

---

## 🛒 Creating Your First Order

1. Go to **Sales** → **Orders**
2. Click **New**
3. Select a customer (or create new one)
4. Add products:
   - Search for products
   - Select and enter quantities
5. Review totals
6. Click **Save**

**💡 Tip**: You can scan barcodes to quickly add products!

---

## 💰 Recording Payments

1. Open the order details
2. Click **Add Payment**
3. Enter:
   - Payment amount
   - Payment method
   - Reference number
4. Click **Save**

---

## 🏪 Cash Register Basics

### Opening a Session

1. Go to **Inventory** → **Shops**
2. Select a shop
3. Click **Cash Register Details**
4. Click **Open Session**
5. Enter opening cash amount
6. Click **Open**

### Closing a Session

1. Open cash register details
2. Click **Close Session** on active session
3. Enter closing cash amount
4. Click **Close**

---

## 📊 Quick Navigation Tips

- **Search**: Use search bars to quickly find products, orders, or customers
- **Filters**: Use filter options to narrow down lists
- **Export**: Click Export to download data as Excel or PDF
- **Notifications**: Check the bell icon for important alerts

---

## 🔔 Understanding Notifications

Click the **bell icon** in the top bar to see:
- **Stock Alerts**: When products are low or out of stock
- **New Orders**: When customers place orders
- **Session Alerts**: Cash register session events

**💡 Tip**: Mark notifications as read to keep your inbox clean!

---

## ⚙️ Common Settings (Admin Only)

Go to **Administration** → **Settings** to configure:
- **Low Stock Threshold**: When to alert about low stock
- **Currency**: System currency
- **Tax Rate**: Default tax percentage
- **Return Window**: Days allowed for returns

---

## 🎯 Role-Based Access

Your access depends on your role:

- **ADMIN**: Full access to everything
- **VENDOR**: Sales, orders, customers, payments
- **WAREHOUSEMAN**: Inventory, products, suppliers, purchases
- **ACCOUNTANT**: Financial documents (read-only)
- **AUDITOR**: Read-only access for auditing
